A product in GoPayLocal represents something you sell. Products are the foundation for campaigns, which define how PPP discounts are applied.

## Creating a Product

<Steps>

1. Go to [app.gopaylocal.com](https://app.gopaylocal.com) and log in
2. Click **Products** in the sidebar
3. Click **Add product**
4. Fill in the product details
5. Click **Create product**

</Steps>

Only the workspace owner and admins can add products (see [Team](/dashboard/team/)).

The product is created in the environment selected in the sidebar switch (Live or Test).

## Product Fields

| Field | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Product Name** | Yes | Display name (e.g., "Pro Plan", "Lifetime Access") |
| **Product URL** | Yes | The page where this product is sold (e.g., `https://yourapp.com/pricing`) |
| **Currency** | Yes | The currency your price is set in: USD or a country's local currency (default: USD) |
| **Base Price** | Yes | The price before any discount (e.g., 49.00), with no more decimals than the currency has (none for JPY); the form won't save a price that doesn't fit |

The base price and currency are used for the price preview when you create a campaign. The banner shows the currency's symbol.

## Product Limits

| Plan | Products |
|---|---|
| Free | 1 |
| Pro | Unlimited |
| Self-hosted | Unlimited |

When the limit is reached, the dashboard says so and links to **Settings > Billing**.

## Importing Products

If a connected payment provider can list its products, you can import them instead of creating them by hand.

1. Go to **Integrations**
2. On the connected provider's card, open **Manage** and click **Import live products** (or **Import test products**, in Test)
3. Select the products you want to import
4. Click **Import**

Product listing works for Stripe, Paddle, Gumroad, Dodo Payments, Creem and WooCommerce. Chargebee, Polar and Whop don't support it; GoPayLocal answers "This provider does not support product listing".

Products are imported into the environment selected in the sidebar. A product already in that environment with the same URL is skipped; a product without a URL is matched by name. Stripe and Paddle don't return a product page, so products imported from them have an empty URL.

## Managing Products

The dashboard lists your products with their currency, base price and creation date. It has no screen for editing or deleting a product; both are available only through the [Products API](/api/products/) (`PATCH` and `DELETE /v1/teams/:orgId/products/:id`), with a secret key or the dashboard session. A secret key, owners and admins can edit and delete any product. Members can edit the products they have access to and can't delete products.

Changing a product's environment is refused (`409`, `product_has_campaigns`) while the product has campaigns. Deleting a product is refused (`409`, "Delete this product's campaigns before deleting it.") while it still has campaigns. Deleting a product also deletes its ended A/B tests.

## Multiple Products

Each product has its own campaigns, so each product can have its own pricing mode, maximum discount and payment integration.

Your API key belongs to the workspace, not to a product. On a page that sells one specific product, tell the banner which one with `data-product` (or `productId` in the SDKs). Without it, the banner answers for the product of your most recently created active campaign. See [Banner installation](/banner/installation/).
