The hosted service has two plans, Free and Pro. Every feature is on both; Pro removes the limits and the GoPayLocal branding. A [self-hosted](/self-hosting/) deployment has no plans, no limits and no billing.

## Plans

| | Free | Pro | Self-hosted |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $0 | $19/month, or $180/year | No billing |
| **Products** | 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **API calls per month** | 10,000 | 20 million (contact us for more) | Unlimited |
| **Team members** | 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Analytics history** | 7 days | 365 days | No limit |
| **"Powered by GoPayLocal" on the banner** | Shown | Removed | Removed |

Pro includes 20 million API calls a month. Nothing is cut off at that number: GoPayLocal's admins are alerted when an organization passes it, and we get in touch about a plan for your volume. If you expect more than 20 million calls a month, contact us first.

<Aside type="tip">
The Free plan has no time limit and needs no credit card. Every feature works on it, including all payment integrations, VPN detection and A/B testing.
</Aside>

## What Counts as an API Call

One API call is one request for a visitor's banner config (`GET /v1/banner/config/:publicKey`) that has a valid public key and reaches campaign selection. It counts whatever the answer is, even when no banner is shown because there is no active campaign, the domain isn't allowed or the country has no discount.

These don't count:

- requests answered `unknown_country`, `vpn_detected` or `quota_exceeded`;
- invalid keys and rate-limited requests;
- loading the script files;
- banner events (impressions, clicks, copies).

Requests answered by a test-mode campaign are recorded separately and don't count. GoPayLocal counts the calls on its own servers; nothing a browser reports changes the count.

The banner script keeps the answer for 5 minutes in the browser tab, so a visitor who views several pages in that time makes one call. See [What counts as an API call](/api/banner/#what-counts-as-an-api-call) for the details.

## When a Free Workspace Runs Out of API Calls

Once a Free workspace has used its 10,000 live API calls in a calendar month (UTC), the banner shows nothing for the rest of the month: the config endpoint answers `show: false` with `reason: "quota_exceeded"`. Your site keeps working. Usage is recomputed every 10 minutes, so the cutoff can come up to about 10 minutes late. The count starts again on the 1st.

**Settings > Billing** shows this month's usage. Each meter turns red above 80%, and a Free workspace at its product or API-call limit sees an upgrade prompt. GoPayLocal doesn't send usage emails.

## Managing Your Subscription

Go to **Settings > Billing**. Everyone in the workspace can see the plan and this month's usage of products, API calls and team members. Only the workspace owner can change the plan.

- **Upgrade to Pro**: choose **Upgrade to Pro · $19/mo** or **Pro yearly · $180/yr**. You pay on a Dodo Payments checkout page and come back to the dashboard.
- **Change or cancel**: **Manage Billing** opens the Dodo Payments billing portal.

On a self-hosted deployment the Billing tab is hidden.

## Payment Processing

Billing is handled by **Dodo Payments**, which acts as the Merchant of Record: Dodo charges you, handles sales tax, VAT and GST, and issues the invoices.

## When Pro Ends

A canceled, past-due or on-hold Pro subscription stays Pro until the end of the period you paid for. After that the workspace is on Free:

- Nothing is deleted. Products, campaigns and team members stay, but you can't add products or invite people beyond the Free limits.
- The Overview shows the last 7 days.
- The banner shows "Powered by GoPayLocal" again.
- The 10,000-call monthly limit applies.
