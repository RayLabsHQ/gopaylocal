GoPayLocal counts four things per day, campaign and country: banner **impressions**, **clicks**, **copies** of a coupon code, and **redemptions**. The dashboard's **Overview** page shows the totals.

## Where the Numbers Come From

| Metric | Recorded when |
|---|---|
| **Impressions** | The banner script or an SDK shows the banner |
| **Clicks** | A visitor clicks the banner's button |
| **Copies** | A visitor copies the coupon code |
| **Redemptions** | Your payment provider reports, through its webhook, an order that used one of the campaign's codes |

Redemptions come only from payment-provider webhooks. A browser can't report a redemption, so redemption counts can't be inflated by someone holding your public key. Dismissals are not counted.

Events are counted as they arrive, and a redemption appears once the provider's webhook reaches GoPayLocal.

## Overview Page

Choose the range at the top: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Last 365 days**.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **API Calls** | The number of banner impressions in the range, with the number of redemptions under it. This is not the metered API-call count; see [Billing](/dashboard/billing/) for that. |
| **Active Products** | The number of products in the selected environment (Live or Test), with your plan |
| **Revenue Impact** | The discount given on redeemed orders, per currency (amounts in different currencies are never added together), with the number of discounts applied |
| **Countries Reached** | The number of countries with events in the range |

Below the cards, **Top Countries** lists each country code with its impressions (**Hits**), most first.

The Overview totals cover all of the workspace's campaigns. Apart from **Active Products**, they don't change with the Live/Test switch.

An [A/B test](/guides/ab-testing/) shows its two campaigns' numbers side by side on its own page.

## Export

**Export** downloads the country breakdown for the selected range as a CSV file with the columns Country, Impressions, Clicks, Copies, Redemptions and Revenue Saved (by currency). It is available on every plan.

## How Far Back You Can See

| Plan | History |
|---|---|
| Free | 7 days |
| Pro | 365 days |
| Self-hosted | No limit |

A longer range than your plan allows shows only your plan's window. See [Billing](/dashboard/billing/).

The hosted service deletes analytics and API-call counts once they are 24 months old. A self-hosted deployment never deletes them.

## Who Sees What

The owner and admins see the whole workspace. A member sees the numbers for the products they have access to. See [Team](/dashboard/team/).

## Privacy

<Aside type="note">
Analytics hold counts per day, campaign and country. They don't contain IP addresses, visitor IDs or page URLs.
</Aside>

- Country-level only: no city or region.
- The banner script sets no cookies. It keeps a random visitor ID in `localStorage` (used to keep a visitor in the same A/B test arm) and the latest banner response and a dismissal in `sessionStorage`. `data-no-storage` turns all of that off; see [Banner installation](/banner/installation/).
